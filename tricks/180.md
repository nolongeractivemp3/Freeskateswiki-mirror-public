# 180
![180.gif](/tricks/180.gif){.align-right}

The 180 is a fundamental trick where you rotate your direction of travel 180 degrees while riding, transitioning from your normal stance into [Switch](/basics/Stances).
## Step-by-Step Breakdown
Turn your front skate outward 90 degrees to get it out of the path of your trajectory.

Drive your back skate forward past your front skate, bringing your feet momentarily into a [Tokyo-style](/tricks/tokyo) position.

Rotate your shoulders aggressively into the turn to pull your lower body around, snapping the skates into the final switch stance.
