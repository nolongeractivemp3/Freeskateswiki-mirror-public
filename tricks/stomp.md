While there is no defenitive date for when the stomp was invented, it is definetly one of the oldest discovered tricks on freeskates. It is done by pushing of one one leg and throwing your skate down under your seccond leg. 

The stomp can be done with both the back and front foot as well as regular and switch

## Media

JMK published a [video](https://youtu.be/If9FfTP7EvA?si=EEOO-LXmGVd6OHoQ) teaching the trick to a beginner

## Tips for learning

 - First practice doing the Stomp without the other skate
 - While doing the stomp, Lift your foot up before dropping the skate
 - While dropping the skate, hold it on the outer edge, not the inner edge
 - Don't let the skate bounce, your foot should hit the ground with the skate
