Riding onefoot is a foundational skill required for a wide variety of intermediate and advanced freeskate tricks, including the [Banana](/tricks/Banana) and [Kickflips](/tricks/kickflip). As you build stability riding on a single skate, you can also learn to maintain momentum by pumping one-footed.

## Tips for learning
- Start on two feet, drop one: Instead of starting from a standstill on a single skate, build up comfortable speed on both skates first and then lift your foot off one skate.

- Arm balance: Keep your upper body relaxed and use your arms for subtle adjustments rather than abrupt leaning.
